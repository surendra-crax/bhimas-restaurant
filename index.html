Our Story

A Taste of Tradition,
A Story Worth Telling

Born from a deep love of South Indian cooking, Bhimas Traditional Indian Kitchen brings the soul of a home kitchen to the heart of Luxembourg City. Every dish is prepared the way it has always been — with patience, whole spices ground fresh each morning, and recipes that have crossed generations.

We believe food is memory. A mouthful of biryani should carry you to a grandmother's kitchen. A bowl of sambar should feel like monsoon rain on a warm afternoon. At Bhimas, we cook to make you feel at home.

Atithi Devo Bhava. View Menu
The Bhimas Experience

Where Every Meal
Is a Celebration

Step through our doors and into a world where time slows down, aromas take hold, and every plate arrives as a small act of devotion. Our chefs draw from regional traditions across India — from the coastal curries of Kerala to the tandoor traditions of the North — creating a menu that tells the story of an entire subcontinent.
